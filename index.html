Nigeria

Nigeria is a country located in West Africa, known for its diverse culture, large population, and vibrant economy. It is the most populous country in Africa, with over 200 million people and more than 250 ethnic groups, including the Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo. Nigeria is rich in natural resources, particularly oil and gas, which play a major role in its economy. The country has a federal system of government and its capital is Abuja, while Lagos is its largest and most economically significant city. Despite facing challenges such as political instability and infrastructure deficits, Nigeria remains a regional powerhouse with significant influence in African politics, culture, and business.
